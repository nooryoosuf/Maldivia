1. Wild Marine Life Sightings

Whale sharks in South Ari Atoll, manta rays in North Ari Atoll, sea turtles on Thoddoo, and stingrays on Fulidhoo are 100% wild marine animals living in the open Indian Ocean. While our featured islands are located in the premier year-round habitats for these species, sightings on any single excursion depend on natural ocean conditions and can never be artificially guaranteed.

2. Official Government Entry Requirements

Entry rules, IMUGA declaration requirements, and tax rates are set by the Government of the Republic of Maldives (Maldives Immigration and MIRA). Travellers should always verify their passport validity before departure.
