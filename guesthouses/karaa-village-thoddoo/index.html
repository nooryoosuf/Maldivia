Karaa Village

Tropical Garden Courtyard Guesthouse with Bicycles on Agricultural Thoddoo Island

Verified Property Overview

In the Maldivian language of Dhivehi, "Karaa" means watermelon—a tribute to Thoddoo Island’s centuries-old heritage as the agricultural fruit basket of the Maldives. Tucked along a quiet sandy street in the heart of Thoddoo, Karaa Village surrounds a lush tropical courtyard shaded by palms, bougainvillea, and woven hammocks.

Because Thoddoo is a broad oval island bordered by farm tracks and palm avenues, Karaa Village provides bicycles so guests can ride effortlessly through the watermelon and papaya plantations to Sunset Bikini Beach, where wild green and hawksbill sea turtles graze daily along the shallow house reef.

Verified Property Amenities & Highlights

  • Garden courtyard restaurant serving fresh Thoddoo tropical produce & seafood
  • Guest bicycles for island and beach exploration
  • Sun loungers and umbrellas at Thoddoo Bikini Beach
  • Snorkelling gear, night fishing, manta ray & sandbank excursions
  • 24-hour front desk & harbour buggy transfer
