Kaani Palm Beach

Beachfront Hotel with Rooftop Infinity Pool on Maafushi Bikini Beach

Verified Property Overview

Operated by Kaani Hotels—one of the foundational hospitality groups on Maafushi Island—Kaani Palm Beach stands directly on the edge of the island’s designated Bikini Beach. Guests step straight from the ground-floor terrace onto soft coral sand shaded by coconut palms, with sheltered turquoise lagoon waters immediately in front of the property.

The property was among the first inhabited-island hotels in the Maldives to introduce a rooftop infinity pool overlooking the Indian Ocean horizon. Combined with its dedicated Kaani Express speedboat fleet operating directly from Velana International Airport (MLE) and an in-house excursion desk organising daily snorkelling and dive trips across South Malé and Vaavu Atolls, it offers a seamless introduction to Maldivian local island travel.

Verified Property Amenities & Highlights

  • Rooftop infinity swimming pool with ocean views
  • Direct beachfront access to Maafushi Bikini Beach with sun loungers
  • Sampa Spa offering Thai, Balinese, and aromatherapy massages
  • 24-hour front desk & multilingual concierge team
  • In-house excursion, water sports, and PADI diving desk
  • Elevator access across floors
  • Complimentary Wi-Fi throughout the property

Dining & Accessibility Information

Palm Beach Restaurant & Rooftop Café

Serves daily Continental, Asian, and traditional Maldivian breakfast (including Mas Huni with freshly baked Roshi flatbread), alongside à-la-carte lunches and beachfront seafood barbecue buffets in the evening.

Dietary & Halal Note: All meats and poultry served are 100% Halal in accordance with Maldivian law. Vegetarian options and Chinese-friendly hot dishes are available; no alcohol is sold on inhabited local islands.

Accessibility Information

Equipped with passenger elevator access between floors and step-free beachfront terrace access; guests with specific mobility requirements are encouraged to mention their needs during enquiry so ground- or lower-floor rooms near the lift can be confirmed.
