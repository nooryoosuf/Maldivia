Coral Reef View Inn

Steps from Ukulhas’ 1 km Bikini Beach & Shore-Entry House Reef in North Ari Atoll

Verified Property Overview

Situated on Vasha Magu along the western shoreline of eco-awarded Ukulhas Island, Coral Reef View Inn places guests within footsteps of the white coral sands of Ukulhas Bikini Beach. Unlike islands where boat trips are required every time you wish to see coral, Ukulhas features a thriving shore-entry house reef directly in front of the beach.

Run by experienced Ukulhas hosts, the inn provides dedicated beach sunbeds and umbrellas, fresh yellowfin tuna dining (celebrating Ukulhas’ reputation as a premier tuna-fishing island), and boat excursions to Ukulhas Thila Manta Point and nearby uninhabited sandbanks.

Verified Property Amenities & Highlights

  • On-site restaurant with indoor and alfresco garden dining
  • Reserved beach sunbeds and umbrellas on Ukulhas Bikini Beach
  • Guided manta ray, coral garden, and sandbank excursions
  • Traditional handline and big-game tuna fishing trips
  • Harbour meet-and-greet & complimentary Wi-Fi
