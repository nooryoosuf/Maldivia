Reveries Diving Village

Southern Atoll Boutique Dive & Surf Hotel on Gan Island, Laamu Atoll

Enquire About This Property
Photo by Пчерка / Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0)

Verified Property Overview

Located just two degrees north of the Equator on Gan Island in Laamu Atoll, Reveries Diving Village pioneered boutique community-integrated tourism in the southern Maldives. Fronting the vast, uncrowded expanse of Thundi Beach, the property combines full hotel amenities—including both an outdoor swimming pool and an indoor pool, a spa, and a 24-hour gym—with direct access to some of the most pristine coral reefs in the archipelago.

One of Reveries’ unique advantages for travellers sensitive to sea swell is its transfer route: after a scenic 40-minute domestic flight from Velana International Airport (MLE) to Kadhdhoo Airport (KDO), guests travel by air-conditioned car across the scenic causeway road directly to the hotel without needing a speedboat transfer.

Accessibility Information

Because transfers from Kadhdhoo Airport (KDO) to Reveries Diving Village take place entirely by road vehicle across the bridge causeway, guests avoid stepping onto wet boat jetties or speedboats.
