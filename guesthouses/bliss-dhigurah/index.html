Verified Property Overview

Positioned on Dhigurah Island alongside the South Ari Marine Protected Area (SAMPA), Bliss Dhigurah was created for independent travellers and ocean lovers seeking boutique hotel refinement on a quiet local island. Just steps away through a lush palm path lies Dhigurah’s western lagoon beach, where complimentary loungers face three kilometres of powder-white sand.

On-site, Hermit’s Restaurant & Bakery prepares freshly baked sourdough bread, wood-fired pizzas, homemade gelato, and Maldivian seafood curries daily. The property’s excursion and PADI dive team leads regular boat trips along the outer Maamigili-Dhigurah reef shelf—globally celebrated for year-round whale shark encounters—while prioritising eco-conscious practices such as solar water heating and refillable glass water bottles.
