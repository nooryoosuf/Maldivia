Mobile Connectivity, Tourist eSIMs & Island Wi-Fi

4G/5G coverage across inhabited islands via Dhiraagu and Ooredoo Maldives, airport tourist SIM pickup, and WeChat / international app access.

Photo by Ishan (@seefromthesky) / Wikimedia Commons (CC0)

KEY VERIFIED TAKEAWAYS

  • Inhabited islands have dedicated telecom towers providing reliable 4G/5G mobile data across the island and nearby lagoons.
  • Official Dhiraagu and Ooredoo tourist SIM/eSIM counters are located immediately outside Customs at Velana Airport (MLE).
  • WeChat, WhatsApp, Alipay messaging, Google Maps, and international websites work without restriction in the Maldives.

Guesthouse Wi-Fi vs. Tourist Data SIM

All six verified guesthouses provide complimentary Wi-Fi in rooms and dining areas. However, purchasing a tourist data SIM or eSIM at Velana Airport is highly recommended so you remain connected during speedboat transfers and boat excursions.
