Thoddoo Island

Known as the green agricultural heart of the Maldives, this oval coral island pairs shaded bicycle lanes and tropical fruit plantations with two expansive Bikini Beaches and a resident sea turtle reef.

Plan a Stay on This Island
Photo by Alessandro Caproni / Wikimedia Commons (CC BY 2.0)

Island Profile & Geography

Unlike narrow ribbon islands, Thoddoo is a broad, fertile oval island standing on its own oceanic reef north of Rasdhoo Atoll. Roughly one-third of the island houses the village and boutique garden guesthouses, while the surrounding lands are cultivated with watermelon, papaya, passionfruit, and coconut plantations.

Visitors explore Thoddoo primarily by bicycle along sandy avenues shaded by towering palms. Both the western Sunset Bikini Beach and eastern Sunrise Bikini Beach offer wide arcs of powder-fine sand, and the western house reef is renowned for resident green and hawksbill sea turtles.

Notable Island Landmarks & Reefs

Thoddoo Western Sunset Bikini Beach & Turtle Reef

A wide, palm-fringed beach where sea turtles regularly feed along the shallow reef edge just offshore.

Thoddoo Agricultural Plantations

Shaded farm tracks where local growers cultivate the Maldives’ famous sweet watermelons and papayas.

Available Activities & Marine Excursions

  • Shore-entry snorkelling with green and hawksbill sea turtles on Thoddoo House Reef
  • Cycling through Thoddoo’s watermelon, papaya, and coconut plantations
  • Visiting the pre-Islamic Buddhist temple ruins (Thoddoo Dagaba)
  • Manta ray and Rasdhoo Atoll reef excursions by boat
  • Sunset relaxation on the western Bikini Beach palm swings

Essential Visitor Information
Bikini Beach:Two designated Bikini Beaches (Western Sunset Beach and Eastern Sunrise Beach).
House Reef:Direct shore-entry house reef off Sunset Bikini Beach with high sea turtle density.
ATM & Currency:Bank of Maldives (BML) ATM located in the village; USD cash and cards accepted at guesthouses.
Medical Facility:Thoddoo Health Centre operates 24/7 in the main village.
Local Etiquette:When cycling between the village and the Bikini Beaches, please wear a shirt/cover-up over swimwear.
