Maafushi Island

The pioneer of local island hospitality in the Maldives, situated 30 minutes by speedboat from Velana Airport with beachfront dining, PADI dive centres, and rapid access to South Malé Atoll reefs.

Plan a Stay on This Island

Island Profile & Geography

Following the Maldives government’s 2009 reform permitting guesthouses on inhabited islands, Maafushi in Kaafu Atoll became the country’s flagship community tourism hub. Positioned just 27 kilometres south of Velana International Airport, it removes the need for domestic flights while offering immediate access to the turquoise lagoons and outer channels of South Malé Atoll.

The northern end of Maafushi centres around its palm-shaded Bikini Beach, oceanfront cafés, and properties such as Kaani Palm Beach. Daily excursion boats depart from the main jetty to nearby sandbanks, nurse shark snorkelling sites in Vaavu Atoll, and world-renowned drift dive sites including Kandooma Thila and Cocoa Corner.

Notable Island Landmarks & Reefs

Maafushi Northwestern Bikini Beach

A sheltered white-sand bay enclosed by breakwater reefs, offering calm swimming conditions and beachfront loungers.

Kandooma Thila & Kuda Giri Wreck

Located a short boat ride south of Maafushi, featuring grey reef sharks, eagle rays, and an intact submerged cargo wreck surrounded by glassfish.

Available Activities & Marine Excursions

  • Snorkelling safaris to Biyadhoo Reef and Turtle Reef
  • Scuba diving at Kandooma Thila, Cocoa Corner, and Kuda Giri Wreck
  • Half-day sandbank excursions in South Malé Lagoon
  • Sunset traditional handline fishing by dhoni
  • Motorised and non-motorised lagoon water sports

Essential Visitor Information
Bikini Beach:Designated Bikini Beach on the northwestern shoreline with direct access from beachfront hotels.
House Reef:Calm shallow lagoon off the beach; best coral and reef fish diversity accessed via 10–15 minute boat trips to nearby reefs.
ATM & Currency:Bank of Maldives (BML) ATM located on the island dispensing MVR; USD cash and major credit cards widely accepted.
Medical Facility:Maafushi Health Centre operates 24 hours on the island, with rapid speedboat access to Malé hospitals.
Local Etiquette:Modest dress (covering shoulders and knees) is required when walking through the central village streets and harbour.
