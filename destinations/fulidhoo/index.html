Fulidhoo Island

An intimate, car-free coral island on the northern rim of Vaavu Atoll where wild stingrays glide into the shallow shore and world-famous channel dives lie minutes away.

Plan a Stay on This Island
Photo by Ahmed Saffu / Wikimedia Commons (CC0)

Island Profile & Geography

Walkable from end to end in under ten minutes, Fulidhoo offers a tranquil counterpoint to larger islands. Its sandy lanes are shaded by breadfruit and banyan trees, with zero motor cars and a deeply rooted cultural heritage celebrated through traditional Langiri and Bodu Beru music.

Along the southern jetty shoreline, wild pink whipray stingrays and nurse sharks regularly patrol the knee-deep water. Offshore, Vaavu Atoll’s eastern channels—such as Miyaru Kandu and Alimatha—offer some of the highest concentrations of nurse sharks and pelagic life in the central Maldives.

Notable Island Landmarks & Reefs

Fulidhoo Southern Lagoon Shoreline

Clear shallow waters by the arrival jetty where wild stingrays and nurse sharks swim freely up to the shoreline.

Eastern Crescent Bikini Beach

A quiet, palm-backed white coral beach facing a calm turquoise lagoon ideal for swimming.

Available Activities & Marine Excursions

  • Snorkelling with wild nurse sharks at Shark Point in Vaavu Atoll
  • Observing wild whipray stingrays along the Fulidhoo shoreline
  • Scuba diving at Miyaru Kandu, Fotteyo Kandu, and Alimatha
  • Excursions to the half-submerged Keyodhoo Shipwreck and sandbanks
  • Experiencing authentic Fulidhoo Langiri cultural drum performances

Essential Visitor Information
Bikini Beach:Dedicated Bikini Beach situated along the eastern end of the island.
House Reef:Accessible from the northern and eastern reef edges with healthy table corals and reef sharks.
ATM & Currency:No ATM on Fulidhoo Island; please bring sufficient USD or MVR cash alongside credit cards for incidental expenses.
Medical Facility:Fulidhoo Health Centre provides basic medical care; regional hospital located on Felidhoo within the same atoll.
Local Etiquette:Fulidhoo is a small, close-knit community; guests are kindly asked to wear t-shirts and shorts/skirts outside the Bikini Beach.
