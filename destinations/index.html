ATOLL GEOGRAPHY

Five Maldivian Atolls Compared

Kaafu (K)27 km south of Malé

Kaafu Atoll (South Malé Atoll)

Located immediately south of Velana International Airport across the Vaadhoo Kandu channel, South Malé Atoll combines rapid 30-minute speedboat accessibility with deep oceanic channels, soft-coral thilas, and the Maldives’ most established inhabited island tourism infrastructure.

Alif Dhaal (ADh)96 km southwest of Malé

Alif Dhaal Atoll (South Ari Atoll)

Home to the South Ari Marine Protected Area (SAMPA), South Ari Atoll is one of the very few places on Earth where juvenile whale sharks aggregate along the outer reef shelf throughout all twelve months of the year.

Vaavu (V)57 km south of Malé

Vaavu Atoll (Felidhoo Atoll)

With one of the smallest resident populations in the Maldives, Vaavu Atoll is prized for its quiet coral villages, shallow lagoons frequented by wild whipray stingrays and nurse sharks, and dramatic eastern channel dives.

Alif Alif (AA)67–72 km west-northwest of Malé

Alif Alif Atoll (North Ari Atoll)

Encompassing both the main northern Ari reef rim and isolated oceanic islands like Thoddoo, North Ari Atoll features sea-turtle-rich house reefs, manta ray cleaning stations, and pioneering community environmental stewardship.

Laamu (L)250 km south of Malé

Laamu Atoll (Haddhunmathi Atoll)

Situated just two degrees north of the Equator in the southern Maldives, Laamu Atoll features the country’s largest island chain connected by an 18 km causeway, ancient pre-Islamic archaeological mounds, world-class reef-pass surf breaks, and uncrowded channel diving.
