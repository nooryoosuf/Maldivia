Dhigurah Island

A slender 3.8 km ribbon of palm forest and powder-white sand bordering the South Ari Marine Protected Area—the premier year-round whale shark sanctuary in the Maldives.

Plan a Stay on This Island
Photo by Syced / Wikimedia Commons (CC0)

Island Profile & Geography

In Dhivehi, Dhigurah translates simply to "Long Island." Stretching nearly four kilometres along the southeastern rim of South Ari Atoll, the island’s compact village occupies the northern harbour area, leaving the remaining three kilometres covered in dense coastal jungle bordered by an uninterrupted western lagoon beach.

Dhigurah’s outer ocean reef forms part of the South Ari Marine Protected Area (SAMPA). Nutrient-rich upwellings along this southern shelf attract whale sharks year-round, making Dhigurah the most reliable base in the Maldives for ethical whale shark snorkelling and diving alongside reef manta rays.

Notable Island Landmarks & Reefs

Dhigurah Thundi (Southern Sandbank)

A natural ribbon of pure coral sand extending over a kilometre from the southern tip of the island toward neighbouring Lux* South Ari Atoll.

South Ari Marine Protected Area (Maamigili Beyru)

Located minutes by dhoni from Dhigurah harbour, this outer reef edge is internationally recognised for year-round sightings of Rhincodon typus (whale sharks).

Available Activities & Marine Excursions

  • Year-round whale shark snorkelling and diving along South Ari MPA
  • Sunset walk along Dhigurah Thundi (southern natural sandbank)
  • Scuba diving at Kuda Rah Thila and Broken Rock
  • Manta ray cleaning station excursions in Ari Atoll lagoon
  • Jungle trail walking from the northern village to the southern tip

Essential Visitor Information
Bikini Beach:Over 2.5 kilometres of designated Bikini Beach along the western sunset coast.
House Reef:Accessible along the western lagoon drop-off with reef fish, turtles, and eagle rays.
ATM & Currency:BML ATM available near the harbour; guesthouses and dive centres accept USD and major credit cards.
Medical Facility:Dhigurah Health Centre is located in the northern village.
Local Etiquette:The northern village area around the harbour and mosque requires modest attire; swimwear is restricted to the western Bikini Beach and southern sandbank.
